# Diction reference (Law 4)

Load this at step 4 of the procedure, never before. Laws 1 through 3 run first, over the full text, without it. Reading this list early is how a reading pass degrades into a word search: the list is the one part of the skill that can be mistaken for the audit.

The entries below are a cache, not a specification. Most are instances Law 3.3 (use the verb the code executes) and Law 3.4 (transaction verbs require physical objects) already generate. Clearing every line here proves nothing about the structural laws.

The banned-at-generation list stays in `SKILL.md`, because those words must be absent when prose is written, not caught when it is reviewed. Its words also appear in the categories below. That duplication is deliberate: this file is the working list at step 4, and dropping them would leave a hole in it.

---

## Categories

- **Structural & Tactile Metaphors:** `load-bearing`, `scaffolding`, `texture` / `adds texture`, `tapestry`, `throughline`, `linchpin`, `bedrock`, `fractal`.
- **Manufactured Profundity & Hype:** `delve`, `robust`, `seamless`, `leverage`, `nuance` / `nuanced`, `salient`, `poignant`, `elegant`, `compelling`, `trenchant`, `visceral`, `testament to`, `pivotal`, `crucial`, `underscores the importance`, `intricate`, `meticulous` / `meticulously`, `realm`, `encompassing`, `comprehensive`, `innovative`, `garnered`, `boasts`, `aligns with`.
- **Thesis-Framing & Debate Crutches:** `At its core`, `At its heart`, `Crucially`, `It is worth noting that`, `The key insight here is`, `Sits at the intersection of`, `Speaks to [a broader pattern]`, `Fundamentally`, `Notably`, `steelman` / `steelmanning`. Address counterarguments directly without announcing the posture.
- **False-Dichotomy Formulas:** `It's not just X, it's Y` / `Less about X and more about Y`.
- **Rule of Three:** Triads of adjectives, verbs, or clauses where the third adds no fact. Cut to the terms that carry information.
- **Academic Dynamic Verbs:** Replace metaphorical verbs (`interrogate`, `unpack`, `illuminate`, `crystallize`, `resonate`, `grapple with`) with direct mechanical verbs (`inspect`, `explain`, `clarify`, `resolve`).
- **Padding Participles:** `ensuring`, `highlighting`, `fostering`, `indicating`, `showcasing`, `emphasizing`, `demonstrating`.
- **Copula Avoidance:** Machine prose reaches for a dynamic verb where `is` carries the fact. Replace `serves as`, `stands as`, `represents`, `functions as`, `holds`, `carries`, `keeps`, `claims a success` with `is`, `contains`, `is marked`, `preserves`, `executes`, `reports success`.
- **Motion Verbs for a Static Result:** A value is preserved or is empty; it does not travel back. Replace `comes back unchanged`, `ends up empty`, `comes out plain` with `is preserved`, `is empty`, `is plain`. The motion framing is creative-writing register on a state description, and it reads as narration where the reader wants a fact. Prefer the agentless passive over an active `returns unchanged`: the fact is the invariant (nothing modified it), not the mechanism that handed it back.
- **Emphatic Auxiliaries:** `did bump`, `does hold`, `is in fact` where the plain tense already carries the fact (`bumped`, `holds`). Reserve `do`-support for a genuine contrast the reader would otherwise miss.
- **Conversational Throat-Clearing:** `Basically`, `Note that`, `In order to`, `Let's dive in`, `Here's what you need to know`, `The reality is`.
- **Case Sweeps:** `whichever`, `whatever`, `no matter how`, `regardless of`, `in every case` (when only a single execution path exists).

---

_Literal Exemption:_ Any word above is permitted when used in its strict technical or literal sense (e.g., `unlocks` for a mutex endpoint, `payload` for an HTTP body, `crucial` quoted directly from an upstream RFC).
